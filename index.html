Less setup, more follow-through

A place for the work in between.

Some questions take more than one reply. Talo is built for the research, context, and small decisions that carry from one day into the next — without asking you to manage the machinery.

01

Ask in your own words

Describe the outcome, not the steps. Talo picks the tools and keeps its working visible, so you can see what it is doing and why.

02

Watch it work

It can read a page in its own browser, compare what it finds, and hand off part of the job to a child task you can follow. Take over the browser whenever you like.

03

Pick it back up

Questions, drafts, and finished work wait in the Inbox. Close the window and Talo stays in the tray, so the work does not stop when you look away.

What's inside

One place for the thread to continue.

Conversations, projects, and tools that share context instead of starting over. Every connection is optional and follows the same permission boundary.

Chats with their own context

Keep conversations apart by project. A workspace carries a brief and notes, so every chat in that space starts from what you already know.

Research you can follow

Talo browses in a managed browser. Follow along, take over the wheel, or let it keep going while you work elsewhere.

Google Workspace, on your terms

Read Gmail, work with Calendar, and read or create text files in Drive. Sending, changing, and writing each wait for a one-time approval.

Scheduled tasks

Set a reminder, a report, or a repeatable piece of work. When it runs, the result comes back to your chat instead of disappearing.

Reach it from Telegram

Pair one private account and message your primary chat from your phone. Progress, questions, and approvals come through; other chats keep running.

Local by default

Chats, memory, and settings live on your computer. Provider keys are sealed with Windows DPAPI and never returned to the window.

Clear boundaries

Your work is yours. Connecting is your choice.

Chats and settings stay on this PC. Talo sends only the context a reply needs to the model provider you choose. Google Workspace is optional, its read access runs when you ask, and anything that sends, edits, or creates waits for your approval.

One installer for Windows x64. Talo keeps itself up to date from inside the app, and your chats, projects, and settings are preserved when you upgrade.
