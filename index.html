Less setup, more follow-through

A place for the work in between.

Some questions take more than one reply. Talo is built for the research, context, and small decisions that carry from one day into the next.

Chats with their own context

Keep separate conversations for different projects. Workspaces can hold a brief and notes, so you can return without starting from scratch.

Research you can follow

Ask Talo to look into something online. Follow along in its browser, take control when you want, and come back to the conversation later.

Connected only when useful

Google Workspace is optional. Talo can use Gmail, Calendar, and Drive in a chat, and asks before it sends email or changes your files or events.

Clear boundaries

You choose what to connect.

Your chats and settings are stored on your computer. Talo sends requests and the context needed to answer to the AI provider you configure. Google is optional; content a chat uses may be sent to that provider. Talo asks for your approval before it sends an email or changes a Google event or file.
